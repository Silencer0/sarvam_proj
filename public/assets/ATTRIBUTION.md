# Asset sources

All bundled game models and audio are by **Kenney** and released under **Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal**. Credit is optional under CC0; we include it to document provenance. Files were selected and renamed without changing their media format.

| Pack | Official source | Included content | License notice |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mini Arena | https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-arena | GLB models and their original colormap texture | licenses/mini-arena.txt |
| Impact Sounds | https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds | cast.ogg, hit.ogg, shield.ogg, step.ogg | licenses/impact-sounds.txt |
| Music Jingles | https://kenney.nl/assets/music-jingles | music.ogg, victory.ogg | licenses/music-jingles.txt |

`manifest.json` maps each shipped file to its original pack filename. `music.ogg` is a short pizzicato jingle used as a loop. No generated or proprietary audio or sprite art is bundled.
